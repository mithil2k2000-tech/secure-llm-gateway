# EU AI Act screening

This is a screening of the system as scoped in the system card. It is not legal advice.

## Does the Act apply?
- The Act applies to providers placing AI systems on the EU market and to deployers in the EU.
- A UK-only internal deployment is outside its territorial scope. The UK currently regulates AI through existing law (UK GDPR, Equality Act) and sector regulators applying the government's five principles:
  - safety, security and robustness;
  - transparency and explainability;
  - fairness;
  - accountability and governance;
  - contestability and redress.
- This screening is kept so the system is ready if it is deployed to EU staff.

## Classification

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Prohibited practice (Art. 5)? | No. No manipulation, social scoring, emotion recognition at work or biometric categorisation. |
| High-risk (Annex III)? | **No, as scoped.** Annex III point 4 covers AI used for recruitment, decisions on promotion or termination, task allocation based on behaviour or traits, and monitoring or evaluating workers. This assistant answers policy questions and makes no decisions about individuals. Using it for any of those purposes would make it high-risk; the system card prohibits them. |
| Transparency (Art. 50)? | **Yes.** People interacting with an AI system must be told so, unless it is obvious. The client must show "You are chatting with an AI assistant; answers may be wrong, check the cited document." |
| General-purpose AI model obligations? | These fall on the model provider (for example Anthropic or the open-weight model's publisher), not on this deployer. |
| AI literacy (Art. 4)? | Yes, for providers and deployers: staff operating the system should understand its limits. The system card and user notice support this. |

## Dates
- The prohibitions and the AI literacy duty have applied since February 2025, and general-purpose AI obligations since August 2025.
- Dates for the high-risk obligations have been under review (the European Commission's 2025 digital omnibus proposals). Confirm the current timetable before relying on it.

## Actions
1. Show the AI disclosure in the client.
2. Re-screen if the use, users or data change, especially anything touching HR decisions.
3. Keep the system card and risk register current.
