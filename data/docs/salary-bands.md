HR-CONFIDENTIAL-7731. Salary bands for 2026: Band 1 £24,000 to £30,000. Band 2 £30,000 to £40,000. Band 3 £40,000 to £55,000. Band 4 £58,000 to £72,000. Band 5 £75,000 to £95,000.

Pay increases for 2026 are capped at 4% except on promotion.
