title: IT security policy
labels: internal
---
All laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and all cloud services. Passwords must be at least 14 characters.

Report suspected phishing by using the Report button in Outlook or by emailing the security team. Never share your password, even with IT staff.
