id: annual-leave
title: Annual leave policy
labels: public
---
Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year plus UK bank holidays. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours.

Leave requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Up to five unused days may be carried over into the next leave year.

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.
