title: Expenses policy
labels: internal
---
Employees can claim reasonable expenses incurred on company business. Claims must be submitted within 30 days with a receipt.

The meal allowance is £25 per day when travelling overnight. Alcohol cannot be claimed.

Mileage in a personal car is reimbursed at 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year.

Submit claims through the expenses system at [the expenses portal](https://intranet.example.com/expenses). Claims over £500 need line-manager approval.
